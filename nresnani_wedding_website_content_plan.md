# Nresnani — Wedding Planner Website Content Plan

**3-page website strategy · Elegant & timeless · Portfolio-focused**

The website for **Nresnani** should feel like a refined wedding editorial: romantic photography, graceful typography, warm neutral colors, and thoughtful storytelling. Rather than feeling like a typical event vendor website, it should feel like a curated collection of love stories brought to life.

The primary goal is to let visitors experience Nresnani's work, understand its planning expertise, and imagine what their own celebration could look like.

## Brand direction

### Brand tagline
**Thoughtfully planned. Beautifully remembered.**

### Color palette

| Color | Hex |
|---|---|
| Warm ivory | `#F4EFE7` |
| Champagne | `#D8C6B2` |
| Taupe | `#A99B8B` |
| Espresso | `#42382F` |

### Typography
- **Headings:** Elegant editorial serif, such as Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display.
- **Body:** Clean sans serif, such as Lato or DM Sans.
- **Photography:** Natural light, genuine emotion, refined details, and a consistent warm color grade.

**Brand personality:** graceful, personal, detail-oriented, welcoming, and quietly luxurious.

# How the three pages work together

| Page | Role | Main objective |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Inspire | Build an emotional connection and preview the brand's best work. |
| **Services** | Build confidence | Show planning expertise, explain the process, and clarify available services. |
| **Portfolio & Contact** | Convert | Show real wedding stories, establish credibility, and invite inquiries. |

## Final recommendations for Nresnani

1. **Prioritize real wedding photography.** For a portfolio-focused business, authentic projects will be more persuasive than decorative stock imagery.
2. **Show the story behind the photos.** Include the couple's vision, the design choices, and the details Nresnani helped coordinate.
3. **Keep the visual identity consistent.** Use the same typography, warm neutral palette, image treatment, and understated buttons throughout.
4. **Make inquiries easy.** Keep a visible contact CTA in the navigation and at the end of each page.
5. **Use honest, verifiable content.** Add actual testimonials, completed wedding projects, service details, and team credentials as they become available.

**Overall creative direction:** An elegant, editorial wedding portfolio supported by a clear, welcoming service experience — a website that makes couples fall in love with Nresnani's work before they even make contact.
